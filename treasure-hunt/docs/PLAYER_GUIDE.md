# Player Guide — for the treasure hunter 🗺️

*(Print this, fold it into an envelope, or just read it to her.)*

Someone who loves you very much has hidden a treasure — and the only way to
it is through **twelve riddles across four days**.

## How to play

1. **Sign in** at the link he gave you, with the email & password he set up.
   That's it — no registration, this world only has room for two.
2. Your **map** (the home screen) shows the four days of the hunt, your
   progress (e.g. *7 / 12*), your streak, and your badges.
3. Three riddles unlock each day — at **10:00**, **14:00** and **21:00**.
   A large countdown ticks toward the next one and unlocks it by itself.
   No refreshing needed.
4. Open the current riddle: read the story, look at the photos, play the
   music… then answer the question. Stuck? Tap *“Whisper me a hint…”*.
5. Your answer goes to **him** for review — the moment he decides, your
   screen updates by itself. Approvals come with confetti.

## If you get it wrong… 😏

A wrong answer earns you a **forfeit** — sing, dance, pose, rhyme, whatever
he decrees. You must upload **proof** (text, photo or video — it stays
private between you two). Only when he approves your proof does the
**retry** unlock. Then answer again — properly this time.

## Between riddles

- **Our Story** — a timeline of the two of you.
- **Memories** — a gallery to wander (tap any photo for fullscreen).
- **Notes** — leave him secret notes; he leaves you some too, and a few may
  appear only after certain riddles…
- **Badges** — earned automatically as you play.

## The end

When the twelfth riddle falls, the **Final Treasure** unlocks. Take your
time with it. Bring tissues.

Good luck, clever heart. ❤
